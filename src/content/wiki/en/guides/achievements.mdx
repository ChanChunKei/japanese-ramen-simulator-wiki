## First actions and customization

The early action achievements ask you to serve ramen, beer and gyoza, finish a day, apply a My Design, customize at the locker and expand the shop. They do not describe a particular speedrun order.

“The Perfect Pour” requires serving beer once. Its name is not evidence of a perfect foam ratio or a five-star drink. The separate [beer guide](/cooking/beer/) explains the actual launch-patch fill threshold and foam comments.

For the two appearance tasks, use [layout and My Designs](/restaurant/layout-and-my-designs/). Applying a design and changing the owner’s appearance at a locker are distinct published conditions; doing one should not be treated as proof that the other has been completed.

## Levels, days and sales

Shop Level 5, 10, 15 and 20 each have a named achievement. The ten-day requirement counts business days. These conditions do not tell us which ingredient, employee or machine unlocks at those levels.

The [verified unlock reference](/restaurant/shop-level-and-unlocks/) instead uses explicit purchase and order thresholds from the Day 1 patch. Keep the achievement checklist and ingredient unlock table separate.

“The First Step to Success” is published as **$10,000 cumulative sales**. That means the stated sales milestone, not a required $10,000 cash balance or $10,000 profit. We have not established a payout formula, a regional currency conversion or a guaranteed number of days to reach it.

## Four boss challenges

The named challenges are Ramen Critic, Wealthy Customer, Monk and True Ramen Critic. Each has a separate achievement for clearing its challenge.

The [critic and boss guide](/restaurant/food-critic/) explains the confirmed before-opening invitation condition. The Day 1 patch adds a first-clear cash reward for each boss, but does not publish its amount. No fixed winning recipe, unlock level or minimal route is asserted here.

## A practical completion checklist

This is editorial planning advice rather than a verified fastest route:

1. Tick off the first-dish and first-day actions during ordinary service.
2. Treat My Designs, locker customization and expansion as separate tasks.
3. Review the published shop-level and business-day milestones as the shop progresses.
4. Record each named boss clear independently.
5. Check the other 17 requirements before expecting the final completion achievement.

“The Works, with Extra Everything” requires the other 17 achievements. It does not name a separate secret dish or boss.

If an achievement does not trigger when expected, record the exact requirement, current version and action performed. The published list confirms what is requested; it does not establish every tracking bug, difficulty restriction or co-op eligibility rule. We have not tested whether all achievements unlock for guests or whether any require a new save after an update.

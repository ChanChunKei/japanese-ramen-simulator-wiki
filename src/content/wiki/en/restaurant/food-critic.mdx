## When can I invite a food critic?

Critics can be invited before opening for business. The launch patch adds an explanation when the invitation is unavailable because of that condition. Prepare the kitchen and make the invitation during the before-opening stage; an exact invitation-menu path or complete set of other requirements is not verified in the checked material.

The [Day 1 update](https://steamcommunity.com/ogg/4968640/announcements/detail/702155760121940292) establishes the timing. If an invitation is unavailable, read the current explanation before changing ingredients or assuming the challenge is broken. The announcement does not establish that every named boss uses an identical invitation process.

Before beginning, use the [ramen preparation guide](/cooking/ramen-recipes/) to organize the ingredients and keep the service area readable. This is editorial preparation advice, rather than a guaranteed way to meet a critic's requirements.

## Which boss customers are officially named?

Four boss challenges are named in the public Steam achievement requirements: Ramen Critic, Wealthy Customer, Monk and True Ramen Critic. The requirements confirm that each challenge can be cleared. They do not disclose these customers' exact recipes, challenge levels, target scores, countdowns or individual ingredient preferences.

The names and conditions come from the [official Steam achievement page](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/4968640/achievements/?l=english). Use the [achievement checklist](/guides/achievements/) to track them without turning the achievement titles into recipe hints. A name such as Monk is not enough evidence to assign dietary preferences.

## How can I prepare for a challenge without a fixed winning recipe?

Follow the challenge's actual request and the feedback shown in your game. The official store describes critics evaluating aroma, umami and toppings, while general ramen quality also involves noodle texture, soup temperature and serving time. These are known considerations; their exact weights and a guaranteed winning combination are not public in the checked sources.

The [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4968640/Japanese_Ramen_Simulator/?l=english) supports those considerations. The launch patch also mentions a topping hint for the critic's ramen when holding ingredients. Use current hints rather than a recipe copied from an unverified preview table.

An editorial preparation routine is to:

1. Check the displayed request or challenge information.
2. Prepare the ingredients before final assembly.
3. Verify the broth, tare and requested toppings.
4. Keep the recipient and finished bowl associated.
5. Read the resulting comment and note the specific issue.
6. Change one relevant part of preparation for a later attempt.

The [broth and tare guide](/cooking/broth-and-tare/) explains bottle handling so a transport stack does not interfere with pouring. The [order workflow](/restaurant/orders-and-tickets/) helps with recipient checks. Neither page supplies an invented critic score formula.

## What reward does a first boss clear give?

The launch patch states that the first successful clear of each boss customer awards cash. It does not state the amount. The achievements separately recognize clearing the named challenges, so track the clear and its achievement rather than assuming every repeat attempt pays the same bonus or that a fixed reward is guaranteed afterward.

This is a first-clear reward statement, not a published repeat-farming strategy. Record the result visible in your game if you want to compare earnings. Unsupported cash amounts, exact temperatures, topping quantities and challenge timers should not determine your preparation or expansion budget.

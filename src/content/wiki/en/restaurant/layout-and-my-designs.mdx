## How should I arrange the kitchen before expanding?

Plan the paths you actually use: preparing ingredients, assembling food, delivering orders and returning dirty dishes. The game officially supports arranging equipment, furniture, storage and decorations. The route-based layout below is editorial advice rather than a measured best floor plan, and no exact spacing or expansion-cost table has been verified.

## How can I test whether the layout works for staff and friends?

Walk the route yourself, then observe a real collection-and-return cycle from cleaning staff. In co-op, ask teammates to try their assigned tasks together. This is an editorial test of access and shared space; the official waiting-position marker helps with placement but does not guarantee every custom floor plan.

1. Place or move the equipment and inspect the displayed equipment name.
2. Check the staff waiting position while placing its dishwasher.
3. Walk from storage to preparation, then to the customer area.
4. Return a dirty dish to the cleaning station.
5. Observe staff movement and revise obvious obstructions.
6. Have teammates repeat their [co-op roles](/multiplayer/co-op-roles/) at the same time.

The [Day 1 patch](https://steamcommunity.com/ogg/4968640/announcements/detail/702155760121940292) fixed staff stopping after failed collection or return, and prevents them walking onto two-burner stoves. See [solo staff behavior](/restaurant/staff-and-solo-play/) for the confirmed tasks. Do not assume those fixes certify every tight passage.

## Which objects support My Designs?

The official description lists posters, noren curtains and ramen bowls as My Designs targets, and also describes custom clothing patterns. The launch update adds support for Wide Noren. Choose the intended target and finish equipment placement first, because design mode cannot be entered while placing or moving an object.

The patch also adjusts the design-selection view so designs are centered in shapes matching their target. This helps choose an appropriate design, but no verified image-size, file-format or import-limit specification is available in the checked sources.

If an object is still being placed or moved, complete that action before trying to switch modes. This is a documented interaction restriction, rather than evidence that the object can never be customized. The update additionally states that the initial shop sign cannot be sold; do not plan on selling it to fund an expansion.

## Which achievements relate to redesigning the shop?

Applying a My Design for the first time and expanding the shop for the first time each have an official achievement. Appearance customization at the locker has a separate achievement. These conditions identify specific actions; they do not require a published expansion size, a particular artwork or a decoration score.

The [Steam achievement page](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/4968640/achievements/?l=english) lists “Living Up to the Sign,” “Room to Dream” and “A New Look for the Owner.” Use the [full achievement checklist](/guides/achievements/) to distinguish those actions from Shop Level milestones. For expansion decisions, test your current routes and observed space needs rather than relying on an unsupported return-on-investment estimate.

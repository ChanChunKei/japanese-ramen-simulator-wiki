## What tools are confirmed for thieves and fires?

The game includes thieves who can take cash-register money and fire events, with shuriken and fire extinguishers identified as tools for dealing with them. Keep the relevant tool accessible and follow the current interaction prompts. Exact event probabilities, tool costs, recovery amounts and fire-control timings are not published in the checked sources.

These event and tool descriptions come from the [official Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4968640/Japanese_Ramen_Simulator/?l=english). The [Day 1 update](https://steamcommunity.com/ogg/4968640/announcements/detail/702155760121940292) adds a specific thief fix, rather than a complete strategy or an assurance that theft can always be prevented.

## Does closing the cash register prevent a thief from stealing?

A closed register is not a verified anti-theft guarantee. In a launch-day Steam discussion, a player reported theft even with the register closed and described events while the shop was closed or while using the computer. Those are player reports, not an official definition of all event conditions.

The [thief discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4968640/discussions/0/590691669351036190/) also contains requests for an option to disable thieves. A requested feature does not establish that the current game has such a toggle. The checked official material supplies no confirmed event-disable menu path.

Use the actual event prompt and available tool rather than relying on the drawer position as proven protection. The patch's post-theft interaction fix does not establish that the cash returns when the thief is repelled. If you are comparing losses, record what the game actually shows.

## How can I prepare the restaurant for an interruption?

Reduce the time spent finding tools by keeping them easy to reach and deciding who handles an event in co-op. This is editorial planning advice. It does not change verified event rules, and no source checked establishes that customer timers pause, that cleaning staff respond, or that every cooking task becomes safe to leave.

1. Identify the relevant emergency tools using current game prompts.
2. Check that furniture and stock do not obstruct the route.
3. In co-op, agree who responds and who keeps track of active orders.
4. When an event appears, read the interaction information before acting.
5. Afterward, recheck food, drinks and outstanding service tasks.

Test those routes through the [layout guide](/restaurant/layout-and-my-designs/). The [co-op role suggestions](/multiplayer/co-op-roles/) can help keep an interruption from making several players abandon the same task. These assignments remain team choices rather than game-enforced jobs.

## Will NPC help or the launch patch remove emergencies?

The verified NPC role is cleaning dishes, trays and empty bottles; it does not establish that staff extinguish fires or repel thieves. The launch patch addresses a particular thief interaction failure, while the official store continues to describe emergency events. Neither source promises an emergency-free shift after hiring staff or updating.

Use the [solo staff guide](/restaurant/staff-and-solo-play/) to plan around the confirmed duties. If an event remains difficult to interact with after updating, an editorial report can include the game version, event location, tool held and visible prompt. That documents the failure without turning a single player's result into a universal rule.
